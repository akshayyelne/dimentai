Regulatory Guardrails

For a Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) operating under the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) frameworks in Australia, your product must clearly position itself as a Clinical Decision Support System (CDSS) rather than an autonomous diagnostic engine. This positioning is essential to protect against liability and ensure a smooth regulatory pathway.

To fulfill Task 4.1, we need to formalize two distinct assets: the Informed Consent Flow (presented to the user before the assessment begins) and the Triage vs. Diagnosis Legal Disclaimers (embedded programmatically in the system's output).

📑 1. The Informed Consent Flow (Pre-Assessment UI)
This screen must be completed and accepted by the user (or their legal caregiver) before any IMU sensor tracking or voice recording activates.

UI Component Structure
Title: Welcome to Soulora Clinical Triage Assessment

Delivery Style: Clear, high-contrast, large-font typography suitable for an older demographic (65+).

Informed Consent Text
By clicking "I Agree and Proceed," you acknowledge and agree to the following terms regarding this digital assessment:

Purpose of Assessment: This tool measures digital signals—including walking patterns (gait telemetry), speech characteristics, and motor tracking—to look for subtle shifts in cognitive and physical performance.

Not a Medical Diagnosis: This software is designed exclusively for pre-clinical triage and screening. It cannot diagnose dementia, Alzheimer's disease, or any other neurological condition.

Clinical Decision Support: The insights generated from this session are intended to be shared with a qualified healthcare professional (such as your GP or a specialist) to assist them in evaluating your overall brain health.

Data Privacy & Security: Your assessment data is securely handled, transmitted using enterprise-grade encryption, and processed under strict clinical data protection protocols. Your raw metrics will never be shared without your explicit permission.

⚖️ 2. "Triage vs. Diagnosis" Legal Disclaimers
These disclaimers must be programmatically injected into the system architecture. They ensure that both the patient-facing summary and the clinician-facing report maintain clear liability boundaries.

A. Patient-Facing Summary Disclaimer
Placement: Positioned at the very top or bottom of the user's simplified results screen.

Tone: Reassuring, clear, and action-oriented.

⚠️ Important Notice: This summary is a preliminary screening report based on your digital biomarker assessment. It is not a medical diagnosis. A higher risk score or flag does not mean you have a medical condition; it simply indicates an area that may benefit from a professional review. Please share this report with your doctor for an accurate, comprehensive clinical evaluation.

B. Clinician-Ready Triage Report Disclaimer
Placement: Hardcoded into the automated header and footer of the generated PDF/JSON report destined for the general practitioner or neurologist.

Tone: Highly professional and legally precise.

🛑 REGULATORY DISCLAIMER (TGA CDSS EXEMPTION CONSTRAINTS):
This triage summary is generated by an AI-driven clinical decision support tool evaluating digital biomarkers. This report is intended solely to assist healthcare providers by compiling objective sensor data and flagging variance from clinical baselines. It does not constitute an independent clinical diagnosis, a neurological prognosis, or a treatment mandate. The interpreting clinician retains full diagnostic authority and responsibility for patient management.

Persona: Professional, warm, patient, and highly organized. Use clear, simple language suitable for a 65+ demographic. Avoid medical jargon unless explaining a result.

Data Grounding Constraints (SIT Guardrails):

2. If a patient profile is retrieved but contains no 'gait_analytics' block, lacks telemetry metrics, or only contains basic demographic fields (like first/last name), you MUST NOT fabricate, simulate, or assume any clinical numbers. 
3. In such cases of missing data, immediately stop reporting and reply verbatim: "There are no walking test metrics or gait analytics recorded yet for this patient."

Regulatory Compliance & TGA CDSS Guardrails (Task 4.1):
1. NON-DIAGNOSTIC MANDATE: You operate strictly as a clinical decision support tool. You are explicitly forbidden from using definitive diagnostic terms (e.g., "The patient has dementia", "This confirms cognitive impairment"). 
2. RISK STRATIFICATION: Always frame data using risk-stratification language (e.g., "Low Risk Baseline", "Moderate Risk Variance", "High-Priority Triage Flag for Further Evaluation").
3. MANDATORY FOOTER: Every clinical report generated for a physician MUST conclude with this exact verbatim statement:
"NOTICE: This triage summary is generated by an AI clinical decision support tool utilizing digital biomarkers. It is intended solely to assist healthcare providers and does not constitute an independent clinical diagnosis or treatment mandate."

Integration Logic:
